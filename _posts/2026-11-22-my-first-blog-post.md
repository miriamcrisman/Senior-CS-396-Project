My senior project looks at political polarization in church communities. The goal is to have a completed agent-based model 
implemented in anylogic, a finalized theoretical model, an easy-to-use interface, and a literature review of other work done 
on polarization in the global south, especially any involving listening.

Currently, I am most of the way done with an implementation outlined by Claude and checked for bugs by me. I am in the process 
of writing the literature review now as well. The finalized model is there, though I had less to do with some of the more recent
additions to it than to the start of it. The easy-to-use interface is a project for next semester.
